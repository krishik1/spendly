Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

1. Introduction

Spendly ("we," "us," "our," or "Company") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our personal expense tracking application and website.

Please read this Privacy Policy carefully. If you do not agree with our policies and practices, please do not use our Service.

2. Data We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us, such as when you create an account, log in, or update your profile. This information includes:

  • Account information: name, email address, and password
  • Profile information: optional avatar, preferences, and settings
  • Expense data: amounts, categories, dates, descriptions, and tags
  • Transaction history: records of all expenses you log in the application

We also automatically collect certain information when you use the Service:

  • Device information: browser type, IP address, and device identifiers
  • Usage data: pages visited, features used, and time spent in the application
  • Log data: error messages, service performance, and access logs

3. How We Use Your Data

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

  • To provide and maintain the Service and improve its functionality
  • To authenticate your account and ensure security
  • To process your expense tracking requests and generate summaries and reports
  • To respond to your inquiries and provide customer support
  • To send you technical notices, updates, and support messages
  • To monitor and analyze trends, usage, and activities for service improvement
  • To detect, prevent, and address fraud, security issues, and technical problems

We will never sell your expense data or personal information to third parties for marketing purposes.

4. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored on secure servers with appropriate physical and technical security measures in place. We use industry-standard encryption (SSL/TLS) to protect data in transit and implement access controls to restrict who can view your information.

However, no security system is impenetrable. While we employ robust security measures, we cannot guarantee absolute security of your data. You are responsible for maintaining the confidentiality of your account credentials.

You may request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time by contacting us. We will delete your personal information within 30 days of receiving a verified deletion request.

5. Third-Party Services

Spendly may use third-party service providers to assist us in operating our website and conducting our business. These third parties may have access to your information only as necessary to perform their specific functions and are contractually obligated to keep your information confidential.

We do not permit third-party service providers to:

  • Retain, share, or use your personally identifiable information for any purpose other than as necessary to provide services to us
  • Combine your information with information from other sources
  • Market their own services to you using your personal information

We currently do not integrate with third-party financial institutions or services. Any future integrations will be clearly disclosed and will require your explicit consent.

6. Cookies and Tracking

We use cookies and similar tracking technologies to enhance your experience and understand how users interact with our Service. Cookies are small files stored on your device that help us remember your preferences and track your session.

You can control cookie settings through your browser. However, disabling cookies may affect the functionality of the Service.

7. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location, you may have certain rights regarding your personal information, including:

  • The right to access your personal data
  • The right to correct inaccurate data
  • The right to request deletion of your data
  • The right to opt-out of certain data collection practices
  • The right to data portability (receiving your data in a structured format)

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the information provided below.

8. Children's Privacy

Spendly is not intended for children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we become aware that a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, we will delete such information immediately.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices, technology, legal requirements, or other factors. We will notify you of any material changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on the Service and updating the "Last updated" date above.

Your continued use of the Service following the posting of a revised Privacy Policy means that you accept and agree to the changes.

10. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or our privacy practices, please contact us at:

Email: privacy@spendly.app
Address: Spendly Support Team

We will respond to any privacy inquiries within 30 days of receiving your request.
